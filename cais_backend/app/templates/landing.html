How CAIS Works

From document upload to actionable report — in 4 simple steps.

1

Upload

Drag and drop your construction documents — plans, RFIs, change orders, submittals, or specifications — into the INTEST ZONE.

2

Inspect

PlanInspector scans every page with computer vision, detecting visual and structural violations such as egress width, fire safety, accessibility, and structural integrity.

3

Match

CodeMatcher runs semantic search against 10,000+ jurisdictions, including IBC, NFPA, Florida Building Code, California Title 24, and international regulations, to pinpoint the exact code being violated.

4

Forensic Facts Dosier

Receive a complete forensic dossier with annotated screenshots, cited code references, and a clear path to compliance — ready for permit authorities, legal teams, and project stakeholders.
